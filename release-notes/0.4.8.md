## Lazy Susan

- **Cubicle in Lazy Susan.** When Lazy Susan is installed, Settings > General has **Add Cubicle to Lazy Susan**. It
  adds a Cubicle group under C: go to or send to a workspace (keyed by its number), windows, stashed windows,
  workspace left and right, new and rename workspace, rename Tab, the grid, and a Snap group. Add it again after an
  update to replace the group.
- **`cubicle leader go|send|windows|stashed`** prints those live lists as Lazy Susan items.
- Lazy Susan counts as a launcher, like Alfred and Raycast: from it, send and snap act on the window you were in.

## Command line

- **`--help` never renames.** `--help` or `-h` after any command shows help; `cubicle rename --help` used to name the
  workspace "--help". Put `--` before a name that starts with a dash.
