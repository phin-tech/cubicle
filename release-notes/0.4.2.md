## Stash

- **Drag a window to an edge to stash it.** Drag it by its title bar against the left, right or top edge and hold it
  there a moment: its Tab appears, and letting go stashes it there. Pull away first and nothing happens. Turn it off in
  Settings > Stash.
- **Dock badges on Tabs.** A stashed app's unread count (or whatever its Dock icon shows) appears on its Tab.
- **Open the next stashed window** with ⌃⌥⌘'. Press it again for the one after; after the last, it tucks away.
- **Previews.** Choose "Clicking its Tab, with a preview" in Settings > Stash, and pointing at a Tab shows a picture of
  its window. Pictures need Screen Recording and macOS 14; without them, the preview shows the app's icon.
- **Torn paper Tabs.** A new look: a sticky note ripped off the pad, its outer edge ragged.
