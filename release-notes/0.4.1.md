## Stash

- **Open on hover or click, hide on mouse-out or click elsewhere.** Settings > Stash now lets you choose how a stashed
  window pops out and how it tucks away again.
- **The minimize button can stash a window.** Pick an edge for it in Settings > Stash, and clicking a window's yellow
  button stashes it there instead (clicking it on a popped-out window tucks it back). Hold any modifier key to minimize
  as usual.
- **⌘-Tab to a stashed window** and it pops out and stays out.
- **Drag a Tab away from the edges** and it turns back into a regular window where you let go.
