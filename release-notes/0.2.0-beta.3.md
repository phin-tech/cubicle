## Choose your own click

The click that opens a window's Send to Space menu is now yours to pick. In **Settings → Keyboard**, click the field
under the shortcut list, then hold the modifier keys you want and click with the button you want, the same way you
record a keyboard shortcut. It needs at least two modifier keys, so ordinary clicks, ⇧-click, ⌃-click and ⌘-click are
never taken over. The default stays ⌃⌥⌘ with either button, and the reset button brings it back.

Its on/off switch moved to the same place, next to the other window-moving controls.
