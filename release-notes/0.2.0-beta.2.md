## Easier ways to send a window

- **⌃⌥⌘-click works now, not just ⌃⌥⌘-right-click.** Hold ⌃⌥⌘ and click any window, with or without a secondary
  click, to get its menu of Spaces. While this is on, ⌃⌥⌘-click and ⌃⌥⌘-drag no longer reach other apps. If
  another tool of yours uses them, turn it off in **Settings → General → Window Moves**.
- **The menu bar has "Move Current Window to Space."** It lists the Spaces on the focused window's display and names
  the window it will move. Pick a Space or press its number.
- **Stage on a real display.** A window being sent waits briefly on another display while its own display
  switches. Normally that's a virtual display Cubicle creates. If you have a spare monitor, pick it in **Settings →
  General → Window Moves → Stage windows on**, and Cubicle won't create the virtual display at all. Windows that are
  already on that monitor still go through the virtual display, as does everything while it's unplugged.
  `cubicle send --via-display` uses it too.
