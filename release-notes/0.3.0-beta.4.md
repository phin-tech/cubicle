## Workspaces are here to stay

Cubicle now runs only on its own workspaces. It no longer switches macOS Spaces at all, and the app says
"workspace" everywhere: Settings > Spaces is now **Settings > Workspaces**, and the menu bar, shortcuts and
`cubicle` messages say workspace too. Your names and bindings carry over unchanged.

### New

- **Trackpad swipes.** Swipe left or right with three or four fingers to switch workspaces. It's on by default; turn
  it off in **Settings > General**.
- **Move a workspace to another display.** **Move Workspace to Next Display** in the menu bar (with two or more
  displays), `cubicle move-workspace`, or a shortcut (⌃⌥⌘M, off until you turn it on in Settings > Keyboard
  Shortcuts) moves the workspace under the cursor, with its windows, name and bindings. Windows keep their place on
  the screen, so a left half stays a left half. It goes to the workspace with the same number on the other display,
  or the first empty one, and won't merge two workspaces.
- **Bound apps' new windows go to their workspace.** If Mail is bound to your Mail workspace, a Mail window you open
  anywhere goes there, even on another display, and you go with it. Windows that are open when Cubicle starts stay
  where they are.
- `cubicle bind workspace` binds an app to a workspace without a zone. `bind space` still works.

### Gone

These only mattered for macOS Spaces:

- The staging display, the experimental virtual display, and their settings. Sending a window is instant now, so
  `cubicle send` no longer takes `--via-virtual`, `--via-display`, `--quick-drag` or `--stay`.
- **Override swipe gesture** and **Animation Speed**. Swipes switch workspaces instead.
- Mission Control and Exposé detection, and **Show on-screen display in Mission Control**.
- `cubicle mc-dump`. `cubicle new-space` now adds a workspace instead of opening Mission Control.

If a display still has more than one macOS Space, the welcome walkthrough tells you so. Cubicle can't see windows on
the extra Spaces, so move your windows to the first Space on each display, then remove the others in Mission
Control.
