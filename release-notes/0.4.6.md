## Alfred

- **Stashed windows.** `cb stashed` lists them: ↩ opens one, ⇥ unstashes it, moves it to another edge or names its
  Tab. **Open the Next Stashed Window** and **Unstash All** are there too.
- **Stash from a window's menu.** In `cb windows`, ⇥ on a window now offers Stash Left, Right and Top.
- **Stacking.** `cb stack`: next or previous window in the stack, swap with a neighbouring zone, pause or resume.

The workflow didn't change, so there's nothing to re-add: the new items come from Cubicle itself.

## Command line

- `cubicle stashed --json`, `cubicle open-stashed [next|--window <id>|--app <name>]`,
  `cubicle rename-tab [name] --window <id>` and `cubicle unstash --all`.
