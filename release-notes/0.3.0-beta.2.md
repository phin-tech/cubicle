## Experimental: virtual workspaces instead of Spaces

This beta tries a different way of switching. Cubicle no longer switches macOS Spaces. Each display gets its own
workspaces (nine to start; `cubicle new-space` adds one, up to 16). Switching moves the other workspaces' windows just
past a bottom corner of the screen and brings the new workspace's windows back. AeroSpace and Spaceman work the same
way.

- **Everything you used for Spaces now switches workspaces.** That's the Space shortcuts, left and right, the grid,
  search, the menu, and `cubicle go`.
- **Moving a window is instant.** Sending a window to another workspace from the grid, the ⌃⌥⌘-click menu or
  `cubicle send` happens straight away, with no trip through other Spaces.
- **Focus follows you.** ⌘-Tab or a Dock click on an app whose window is on another workspace takes you there.
- **New windows** join the workspace showing on their display.
- **Quitting Cubicle** puts every window back on screen. They return to their workspaces the next time Cubicle
  starts.

Before you try it:

- **Use one macOS Space per display.** Remove the extra desktops in Mission Control. Cubicle can't see windows on
  other Spaces.
- **Names start blank.** Workspaces have their own names, so your Space names don't carry over.
- **Trackpad swipes** still switch macOS Spaces, not workspaces.
- **Mission Control** shows the hidden windows squeezed in at the edge of the screen.
- **Native full screen** still gives a window its own Space.

If it doesn't suit you, turn off beta updates in Settings and install 0.3.0-beta.1 again from the download page.

## Stacking

Every zone (the halves, the quarters and full) can hold a stack of windows.

- **Bind** an app, or a single window, to a zone on a Space. Its windows go there when they open, even after the app
  restarts. Use the ⌃⌥⌘-click menu, the menu bar, **Settings → Stacking**, or `cubicle bind left-half`.
- **Cycle** through a stack with ⌃⌥] and ⌃⌥[ (`cubicle cycle`).
- **Swap** two zones' stacks with ⌃⌥⇧ + arrows (`cubicle swap left`).
- **Pause** stacking on a Space with ⌃⌥P (`cubicle pause`), or have it ignore an app (`cubicle ignore`).
  `cubicle layout` shows what's bound where.

Moving or resizing a window by hand takes it out of its stack. The stacking shortcuts are off until you turn them on
in **Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts**.
