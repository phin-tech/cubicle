## Stash

- **Rename a Tab.** Right-click it and choose **Rename…**; its name shows instead of the app's name or the window's
  title. **Use the App's Name** puts it back.

## Alfred

- **Add the workflow from Settings.** Settings > General has an **Add the Workflow to Alfred** button, so you can
  install it (or update it after a Cubicle update) any time, not just in the walkthrough.
