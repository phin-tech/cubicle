## Cubicle

Instant, named, searchable Spaces for macOS. This is the first release.

- **Switch Spaces instantly.** No slide animation: press a key and you are there.
- **A grid of your Spaces.** Press ⌃⌥⌘Space for a grid of the Spaces on the display under your cursor. Arrow keys
  move, Return jumps, number keys go straight to a Space, and a click works too.
- **Name your Spaces.** Give each Space a name in Settings ▸ Spaces, or while you're being walked through. Names show
  up in the grid, the on-screen display and the menu bar, which shows the current Space's name beside its number.
- **Search by typing.** Type in the grid and it finds the Space: "ino" finds "Innopts". Matching is fuzzy, ignores
  case and accents, and leaves out the Space you're already on.
- **Choose your own keys.** Pick the shortcuts for the grid and for moving left and right (and for jumping straight
  to Spaces 1 to 16) in the walkthrough or in Settings ▸ Keyboard.
- **A first-run walkthrough.** It gets the Accessibility permission, adds Spaces for you (it can press the + in
  Mission Control), names them, sets your shortcuts and installs the extras. Reopen it any time from the menu bar.
- **A `cubicle` command.** `cubicle list`, `cubicle go code`, `cubicle left`, `cubicle new-space` and more, for a
  terminal or a script. Settings has a button to install it, and `brew install --cask cubicle` does it too.
- **An Alfred workflow.** Type `@` and part of a Space's name. The walkthrough can add it to Alfred for you
  (it needs Alfred's Powerpack).
- **Updates.** Cubicle keeps itself up to date.

Cubicle needs macOS 13 Ventura or later, and the Accessibility permission so it can switch Spaces for you.

Cubicle builds on InstantSpaceSwitcher by jurplel (MIT licensed), and on the custom Space names Glitchy-Sheep
contributed to it.
