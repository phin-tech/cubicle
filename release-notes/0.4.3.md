## Stash

- **Tabs wiggle when there's news.** When a stashed app's Dock badge appears or its count goes up, its Tab gives a
  quick shake. Catching up (the count going down) doesn't.
- **Choose the Tabs' font.** Settings > Stash > Font: Automatic, System, Rounded, Serif, Monospaced, a few
  handwriting faces like Noteworthy and Marker Felt, or any font you have installed. It's used for sticky notes, torn
  paper and window titles.
